## Files

## What this does

`refresh_dashboard_stats()` currently sends both stdout and stderr to DEVNULL with `check=False`. If `dashboard_stats.py` crashes (ImportError, missing config, broken state.json), the flush silently completes and the cache stays stale. The new version captures stderr and on non-zero exit logs:

    log.warning("dashboard_stats refresh failed (exit=%d): %s", rc, stderr[:500])

Identical structure to `refresh_dashboard_lint()` that S02-T01 already shipped — just retroactively applies the pattern to its sibling.
