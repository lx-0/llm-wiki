# M034-S06-T04 -- Task Plan

**Summary:** One collect verb (the rest of exit criterion 6).
- **The flags:** `wiki collect <name> -- <flags>` hands everything after `--` to the
  collector module's `main(argv)`. That covers screenshots' `--backfill` / `--limit` / …,
  browser's `--source`, tabs' `--backup-dir` and youtube's `--url` / `--inbox` / `--tier`.
- **The entry scripts:** the three by-path scripts (`scripts/scan_screenshots.py`,
  `scan_browser.py`, `scan_tabs.py`) are deleted. T03's table removed their reason (runpy's
  double import).
- **PROCESS.md:** its 11 bare `uv run --project .wiki …` lines become `wiki` commands. On an
  iCloud vault the bare form ignores the engine's environment and rebuilds `.wiki/.venv`
  inside the vault.

## Files

- Modify: `scripts/collectors/cli.py`.
  - The argv splits at the first `--`.
  - With a tail, it imports `collectors.MODULES[name]` and calls its `main(tail)`. Exit:
    what `main` returns, or 0.
  - A collector without a `main` is an error naming the ones that have one.
  - An unknown name is the existing error.
  - `--dry-run` / `--incremental` / `--account` stay for the Registry run, before `--`.
- Modify: `scripts/collectors/scan_screenshots.py`, `scan_browser.py`, `scan_tabs.py`,
  `scan_youtube.py`.
  - `main(argv: list[str] | None = None)` → `parse_args(argv)`.
  - The parser's `prog` becomes `wiki collect <name> --`, so `--help` names the real form.
- Delete: `scripts/scan_screenshots.py`, `scripts/scan_browser.py`, `scripts/scan_tabs.py`.
- Modify: `scripts/cli.py` + `scripts/cli_catalog.py`.
  - `refresh-dashboards` joins the catalogue as a `builtin`, with help text. The Obsidian
    button and PROCESS.md use it, and `wiki help` did not list it.
  - `--dry-run` runs both generators' `--dry-run` (JSON, no write) through the child runner.
- Modify: `tests/test_cli_dispatch.py` (the three entries leave the runpy guard list), and a
  new `tests/test_collect_passthrough.py`:
  - the tail reaches `main(argv)` unchanged, flags included;
  - a collector without `main` errors;
  - the Registry flags still work.
- Docs: `docs/PROCESS.md` (the 11 lines), `docs/engine-layout.md` (the three entry-script
  lines), `docs/FEATURES.md` ("script-mode CLI"), `docs/cli.md` (the collect row,
  refresh-dashboards). Release: `CHANGELOG.md` (0.7.33), `pyproject.toml`, `uv.lock`.

## What this does

```
wiki collect screenshots -- --backfill 7        → collectors.scan_screenshots.main(["--backfill", "7"])
wiki collect browser -- --source firefox         → collectors.scan_browser.main([...])
wiki collect email --incremental                 → the Registry run, as now
wiki refresh-dashboards [--dry-run]              → the dashboard caches (or their JSON)
```
