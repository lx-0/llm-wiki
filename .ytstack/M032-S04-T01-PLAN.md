## What this does

The panel is rebuilt behind the harness, not in place: `panel.html` is a second entry the app
does not open yet, so every commit of S04 keeps the shipped panel usable while the new one grows
under the layout gate. The store is the one source the sections read from (T02 status strip,
T03 Next-up / Recent / footer); it keeps the old renderer's behaviours that mattered — poll only
while visible, restore running state on open, `recentSeen` "new" marking — as plain data.

Header: the five unlabeled icons that needed the hint banner become four labeled nav items
(icon + word) and the settings gear; the vault pill still opens Obsidian. Ask: same hero role,
answer card with the engine's markdown, wikilink chips open the file, copy swaps to a check for
1.5 s, clear hides the card — no instructional text anywhere.

TDD: `markdown.test.ts` first; the store's pure parts are covered through T02/T03 derivations.
