## What this does
`_count_term_matches(term, knowledge_dir)` counts knowledge articles (excl.
index.md) containing the term (case-insensitive, one read/file). After
`_write_fact`, for a `negation` fact, each term whose count exceeds the threshold
emits `log.warning("term '<t>' matches N articles — narrow it?")`. Non-blocking.

correct.py imports stay light (no SDK) — a local count fn, not `_scan_candidates`
from correct_apply (which pulls the SDK at import).
