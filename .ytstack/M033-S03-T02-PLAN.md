## What this does

Same method as T01: the shape table runs once through each of the seven old helpers against
its seam equivalent before the fold (evidence in the summary; the differences are the seam's
tolerance plus the three decisions named above — prompts' malformed-body, agent_spec's
messages, links' trailing-space fence), then every site calls the seam, then the forwarders
with a single call site disappear. Each site's contract that the seam does not express
(`None` for gmeet / verify_report, `SpecError` for agent_spec, raw text for utils, the
2 KB head for menu_context, the generator for links) is preserved at the call site, never by
forking the grammar. `import yaml` and `import re` leave the files that only needed them for
the deleted code (`ruff --select F401`).
