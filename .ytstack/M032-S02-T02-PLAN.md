## What this does

Second half of principle 3: the GUI stops rewriting `"entity pages overdue for dream"` with
regexes (`friendlyHealth`) because the check that knows it is overdue also knows how to say it
in plain English. `title` is optional on the dataclass so the ~40 checks whose `message` is
already readable stay untouched (`to_json` falls back to `message`); `verb` comes from the
catalogue's `verb_for`, so a check with `dispatch_args=["reindex"]` renders a "Rebuild" button
without the app knowing what reindex is. The TUI keeps rendering `message` (operator terms).
`schema` on the doctor payload lets the desktop refuse an engine it does not understand (T04).
