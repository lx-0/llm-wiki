## What this does

1. **Config:** `personal.watched_folders` entries gain optional
   `sensitivity: <string>` (operator-chosen vocabulary, e.g. `private`).
   Validation-only change; free-string by design (vocabulary belongs to
   the operator, enforcement to a future consumer — the
   financial-fact-layer backlog's policy seed).
2. **Stamp-through:** `_resolve` is extended (or a sibling
   `_resolve_entry`) to return the matched watched_folders ENTRY; the
   backend threads `entry.get("sensitivity")` into `_render_answer`,
   which emits `sensitivity: <value>` in the answer frontmatter when
   set. Request JSON untouched (the artifact is the carrier).
3. **Compile propagation:** one generic rule appended to
   `prompts/compile_main.md` — sources without the field are unaffected;
   folder answers (and any future sensitive substrate) get their tag
   carried onto derived articles. Prompt-only: empirical verification of
   the carry is part of T03's lxw e2e.
