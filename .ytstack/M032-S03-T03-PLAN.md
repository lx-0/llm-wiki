## What this does

The two list windows move onto the system; both become light-mode-correct automatically because
every colour is a token. Browse keeps its shape (it worked) and gains the shared `TypeBadge`;
the monospace, letter-spaced footer becomes plain muted text with tabular numbers. Triage keeps
the grouped, confidence-aware review (DECISIONS 2026-06-24) but loses the "Captured from your
sources — keep what matters, dismiss the rest." sub-line and the "Keep → becomes a to-do / filed
for reference" group hints: the group label and the Keep verb already say it. Keep stays the
primary-ish (ok-tinted) action, Dismiss quiet; batch "Dismiss all" stays a quiet text button so it
cannot out-shout a verdict. Pure derivations (`filter.ts`, `groups.ts`) carry the logic and the
tests; the components only render.
