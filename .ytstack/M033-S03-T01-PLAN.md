## What this does

Every site keeps its call-site semantics; only the grammar moves. The tolerance the seam
brings (CRLF, trailing whitespace on a fence line, a closing fence at EOF without a newline,
`---` as a first line followed by anything but a newline no longer counting as a fence) is
decided in the seam's favour — it is the C03 policy; the old helpers' stricter or looser
readings were accidents, not features. Two call-site contracts are preserved deliberately:
the `None` contract of the picture-sidecar splitter (skip on malformed) and the WARNING of
the daily-rollup backfill on a malformed file — both via `parse_strict` + `except
FrontmatterError`. `pin.py` and `cleanup_legacy_daily_roots.py` read one scalar and move to
`field`, which strips quotes like pin's splitter did and additionally drops a trailing
`# comment`. `yaml` imports that served only a deleted helper go (`ruff --select F401`).
The commit message quotes the before/after grep counts (slice rule).
