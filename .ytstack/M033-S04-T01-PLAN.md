## What this does

Exit criterion 6 ("no function ≥ 150 lines in `scripts/` outside generated tables") becomes
a committed, AST-measured gate: today it holds with an allowlist of 20 named functions and
their lengths; every S04 task that splits a function removes its entry; a function that
grows past its recorded length, or a new function that crosses the limit, fails CI. The
allowlist is the slice's burn-down and can only get shorter.
