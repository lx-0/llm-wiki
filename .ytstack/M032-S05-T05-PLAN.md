## What this does

The milestone's exit criterion 8 is a blind learnability score ≥ 8/10 (M029 closed at 6/10 after its own fix round). The method is the `synthetic-users` protocol under the memory's learnability discipline: the personas get the rendered screens in first-run order — Welcome, the menu-bar panel, Overview, Notes, Inbox, Settings (the light captures from `desktop/out/shots/`) — with a situational frame that reveals the situation but never the solution ("a small menu-bar app you installed because a friend said it would help with your notes; nobody has explained anything"), and a task quiz whose key only the distiller sees: what the app is for; how to ask it something; how many notes there are and how many were updated today; whether anything is recording and how to stop it; what it wants you to do first and how; how to add a web page you just read; where the unsorted captures live; where the charts are. Each persona also rates "how confident would you be using this on day one without help" 1–10 and names where they'd give up (the number the gate is measured on, averaged over the novice lenses Sam, Nova, Mateo). Seven personas for divergence: Sam, Nova, Mateo, Riley, Priya (would you keep it installed?), Devin (is anything visually broken?) and Jess with the originality question (distinctive, or an over-used mass pattern — the arc's "reads as vibe-coded" charge). Phase 1 runs each persona in its own agent with nothing but situation + files + questions; Phase 2 hands every transcript to `synthetic-distiller` with the key; the synthesis lists consensus issues (≥ 2 personas) first. One fix round addresses the consensus and the located quiz misses, the harness and tests run again, and the three novice personas re-take the identical blind test on the fresh captures — that re-test is the score. Then `npm run dmg`. The operator look at the installed app is the operator's: the summary records the DMG path and the checklist, and marks the look as pending, never as done.
