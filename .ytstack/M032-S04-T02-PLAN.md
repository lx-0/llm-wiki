## What this does

Principle 1 of the arc: show state, don't narrate it. The old panel's stat line, raw path, yield
sentence, "3 things to tidy up" line and listener card become three tiles of equal size, each
a number + a shape + one word:

- **Library** — `2,024` + a 14-day sparkline of notes added (accent bars, today darker) with
  "+15 today" as the caption. Expanded: +N this week, "updated 35m ago", the Update knowledge
  button (progress bar while running, "Up to date · took 2m" / "Update failed" pill after —
  kept until the panel closes). When the vault is unreadable the tile shows "Can't read" and
  the expansion carries the Open System Settings button.
- **Health** — a ring: full green "OK", or amber/red with the count inside and the word
  "issues" beneath; status colour never alone (count + word). Expanded: each check's
  plain-English title with its engine verb button (`runAction(dispatchArgs)`), running /
  done / failed state from the store, fix hint as text when nothing is dispatchable; the app
  update row (Update app) when the engine reports one.
- **Capture** — two thin meters (mic, system) filled by freshness (fresh = full, decaying to
  empty at the zombie threshold), the state word (Recording / Paused / Check / Starting… /
  Stopping… / Not configured) and a Start / Stop button. Expanded: "mic 20 s ago · system 25 s
  ago" and the process name.

Dataviz rules applied: thin marks, rounded data-ends on the baseline, one hue per job (accent
for magnitude, status palette only for status, always with text), no legend needed (single
series), direct labels, no decoration. Tiles are `<button>`s (keyboard + pressed state).

TDD: `status.test.ts` first — day bucketing across a month boundary, week/today sums, health
levels incl. unknown doctor, capture freshness math + toggling words.
