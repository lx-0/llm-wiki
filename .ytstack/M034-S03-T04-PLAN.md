# M034-S03-T04 -- Task Plan

**Summary:** The merge and the uninstall as pure functions in `scripts/core/agents.py` (split
from the port after T03). Rendered with `render()`, they produce what the bash's `jq` writes,
byte for byte. Nothing calls them yet (T05 does), so there is no release.

- **`merge(config, payload)`** is `merge_into_config`:
  - the top level deep-merges the payload minus its `hooks`, as jq's `*` does: objects merge
    recursively, anything else is replaced, existing keys keep their place, new ones are
    appended;
  - per payload event, the entries that are not wiki-managed stay, then the payload's are
    appended;
  - existing events keep their place, new ones go at the end, and `hooks` is created at the
    end when it is missing.
- **`without_wiki_hooks(config)`** is `uninstall_one`'s filter (T02):
  - wiki commands leave `{matcher, hooks}` groups, and a group the removal emptied goes;
  - a bare entry (Cursor) goes when it is ours;
  - an event array the removal emptied goes;
  - anything that is not the expected shape stays as it was.
- Both use the one grammar (`is_wiki_hook`). A group counts as wiki-managed when its bare
  `command` or any of its `hooks[].command` matches, as in the merge's `wiki_managed`.
