## What this does

Closes the engine→app contract: the app renders what the engine says and never rewrites it.
Eleven files is above the usual smell line, but the contract switch cannot be split without
leaving the panel broken in between (the old renderer would call `.trim()` on a missing `cmd`).
The renderer/cockpit edits are the minimum to keep them working on schema 2 — they are deleted in
S04/S05, no polish. `parseMenu` / `parseDoctor` refuse unknown schemas explicitly (empty lists +
warning) rather than guessing, so an engine that is behind shows "nothing pending" instead of
crashing or mis-dispatching.

TDD: update `menu.test.ts` / `doctor.test.ts` first (fail on `tools` / `title` / schema gate), then
the parsers; then the renderer/cockpit/fixture follow-through verified by the harness.
