## What this does

One fence grammar everywhere. The eight readers that still carried their own opening /
closing-fence rule (a regex, a `startswith` / `find` pair, a strip of the first `---` line)
read through `core.frontmatter` — `split_fence` for the split, `parse` / `parse_strict` for
the YAML, `field` for one scalar — so a `----` line, a CRLF fence, or a `---` inside a value
is read identically by the router, the dashboards, the merges and the migrations. The
widened guard measures exit criterion 5 ("0 private parsers") at line level, allow-listing
the one `---` scan that is not a fence.
