## What this does

Establishes the **lifecycle-test pattern** for S04:

1. A pair of fixtures shows the BEFORE / AFTER state-evolution that the LLM is expected to perform.
2. The pytest validates the *plumbing*: the BEFORE fixture has the open commitment shape the lifecycle rule expects to find; the AFTER substrate provides the resolution signal in a form matching the prompt's resolution-signal categories.
3. The truth-set is encoded in the AFTER fixture's `## Expected lifecycle outcome` block (mirroring the S03 fixture convention).

Validating that the LLM actually performs the demotion is a canary-procedure step (added to the canary runbook from S03-T05). T03 ships the fixtures + plumbing tests; T05 of S03's runbook gets an additional canary section "Lifecycle canary".
