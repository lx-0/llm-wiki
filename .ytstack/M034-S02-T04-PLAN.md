## Commits

1. **Bash record first (pushed before port code).** Three paths not yet in the oracle, each a
   mutating case with a preparation step:
   - `install-shortcut-already-linked`: a second run;
   - `install-shortcut-replaces-other-vault`: a symlink to another vault's `wiki`;
   - `install-shortcut-regular-file`: a regular file at `~/.local/bin/wiki`. The bash
     `rm -f`s it.
2. **The port.** Parity on the five goldens, with three differences decided in the commit:
   - **A regular file at the link path is refused, not deleted.** Exit 1, with a message
     saying what is there and what to do. It is not ours: another program may be called
     `wiki`, and the locked decision says writes outside the vault keep a backup or do not
     happen.
   - **The rc line prints with its quotes,** `export PATH="$HOME/.local/bin:$PATH"`. That is
     what the bash meant; its quoting dropped them (shellcheck SC2140).
   - **"Already linked" compares resolved paths,** so a link through `/tmp` → `/private/tmp`
     is not replaced with its own equivalent.
