## What this does

One timed rollup line, one ellipsis, one swallowed append. Every collector that mirrors an
item into `daily/<date>/<source>.md` builds its line from `daily_capture.rollup_line` (the
stamp, the first line, the 80-char cut) and appends it through `base.append_rollup` (the
rollup is a side-effect that never breaks the primary write); the per-collector shape —
what goes before and after the text — stays where it is, byte-for-byte. The watched-inbox
scan is `base.scan_inbox` called directly. Operator-visible: nothing — the rollup files keep
their lines; a multi-line picture scene now yields one list item instead of a broken one.
