## What this does

Two grammars exist in the engine and both are load-bearing for operator filenames, so the
fold is one implementation with the collectors' grammar as an explicit keyword, not a
semantic merge: changing the collectors to the default grammar would rename every new
transcript whose title carries an umlaut or an apostrophe (`m-glich` → `möglich`,
`don-t` → `dont`), and changing the default to ASCII would orphan facts and takes whose
slug is their identity. Evidence before the switch, on the operator vault (read-only,
counts only in the summary): for every `raw/transcripts/jamie/*.md`,
`raw/transcripts/gmeet/*.md`, `raw/notes/youtube/*.md` the slug segment of the filename is
recomputed from the file's own `title:` (and `channel:`) with
`slugify(…, ascii_only=True)` and compared — mismatches are investigated, not waved through.
`yaml` / `re` imports that served only a copy go (`ruff --select F401`).
